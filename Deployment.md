

          Deployment Process
- Stakeholders, including solution owners and clinical staff, must understand the data science model to ensure its relevance and utility.
- The model is tested in a controlled environment before full deployment, allowing for confidence in its application.

Case Study: Intervention Program
- The program aimed to reduce readmission risk for patients, requiring collaboration between business and clinical teams to translate model results into actionable insights.
- An application was developed for real-time risk assessments, enabling clinicians to identify high-risk patients effectively.

Training and Monitoring
- Training for clinical staff is essential for successful implementation, alongside developing processes for tracking patient outcomes.
- Continuous feedback and collaboration with IT are necessary to refine the model over time and improve its effectiveness.
