
      **Understanding CRISP-DM**
- CRISP-DM is an iterative and structured approach to data mining that helps in data-driven decision-making.
- It consists of six stages: business understanding, data understanding, data preparation, modeling, evaluation, and deployment.

**Stages of CRISP-DM**
- **Business Understanding**: This stage defines the project goals and objectives, ensuring clarity among stakeholders to avoid wasted resources.
- **Data Understanding**: In this stage, data scientists identify data sources and gather relevant data for analysis.

**Modeling and Evaluation**
- **Modeling**: Data scientists create models to uncover patterns in the data, selecting and adjusting models based on the data subsets.
- **Evaluation**: The effectiveness of the model is tested, determining its suitability for deployment based on the results.

**Deployment and Feedback**
- **Deployment**: The model is applied to new data, which may lead to further adjustments in the project based on new insights.
- **Feedback**: After completing the stages, a meeting with stakeholders is held to discuss results and ensure the model meets business needs. 

The CRISP-DM process is cyclical, allowing for revisions as necessary to achieve project goals.
