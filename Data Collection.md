     Data Collection

Data Collection Overview
- After initial data collection, data scientists assess whether they have the necessary data, similar to checking for ingredients when cooking.
- This phase involves revising data requirements and deciding if more or less data is needed.

Case Study Insights
- Data sources for a case study include demographic, clinical, and claims records related to congestive heart failure patients.
- It's acceptable to defer decisions on unavailable data and acquire it later if it proves important for model accuracy.

Collaboration and Automation
- Database Administrators (DBAs) and programmers work together to extract and merge data, eliminating redundancy.
- Discussions on data management can lead to automating processes for easier and faster data collection.
