
     Feedback Process
- Feedback from users is crucial for model refinement and performance assessment.
- The methodology is cyclical, allowing for continuous improvement at each stage.

Case Study Application
- A review process is established to measure the results of a model for congestive heart failure risk.
- Patient outcomes are tracked to evaluate the effectiveness of interventions in reducing re-admissions.

Model Refinement
- The model is refined based on data collected post-implementation and insights gained during the feedback stage.
- Additional data, such as pharmaceutical information, may be incorporated to enhance the model's accuracy.
