

     Course Overview
- You learned to think like a data scientist by tackling real-world data science problems.
- The course covered essential steps: defining a business problem, data collection and analysis, model building, and feedback evaluation.

Methodological Steps
- Emphasis on understanding the question, business goals, and selecting the right analytic approach.
- Methodical data handling: determining requirements, collecting, understanding, and preparing data for modeling.

Model Evaluation and Improvement
- Steps for evaluating and deploying models, incorporating feedback for continuous improvement.
- The iterative nature of the methodology allows for ongoing enhancements based on new data or feedback.

Real-World Application
- A case study demonstrated the application of data science methodology in achieving business goals.
- The methodology added value by integrating data science practices into daily operations, improving outcomes like patient care.

Conclusion
- The course emphasized that a methodology helps in systematically addressing problems and finding solutions.
- Success in data science relies on applying the right tools effectively to solve the right problems.
